# CodeBreak Requiem: Endgame Plan (The Recursion)

*Status: phases 1, 1b, 3, 4 and 6 built (the tower, its floors, the map, the Core's terminal, threat rewards with stakes, and all three ending perks: Merge's, Overwrite's and Terminate's REBOOT; section 10), and the Theorist trial on floor 10. Boss floors, tower pressure and the First Executor are still a plan. It covers the endgame tower after the campaign and the three ending perks tied to it. Builds on `CodeBreak_Requiem_Setting_and_Campaign.md` (the source of truth) and `CodeBreak_Requiem_Act_V_Plan.md`, section 9 (the endings).*

---

## 1. The Idea in Four Lines

After the ending, the Core opens a way **up**: **The Recursion**, a tower of 50 floors, one waypoint each, stacked on a vertical map. Every floor is a randomly generated dungeon that borrows one of the campaign's looks (Act I's ward, Act II's road, Act III's districts, the Null, the Core) and rolls its own threats. Each floor is harder than the one below, every tenth floor holds a boss, and floor 50 is the top. Like Path of Exile's atlas, the map is the game: you see the floors ahead, their looks and threats, and your ending decides how much control you have over them.

**Name:** *The Recursion* (a process that calls itself, one level higher each time) is a working title.

---

## 2. Decision Needed First: What "50" Means

| Option | Floors | Levels | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A (recommended)** | 50 floors | Level 51 at floor 1, one level every 5 floors, 60 at the top | The level 55 item bases already kept for "an endgame past level 50", and the top affix tiers (5 affixes need item level 55, 4 need 60: crit chance, crit multiplier, move speed and skill damage); the Theorist rank, reserved for after the campaign |
| B | 50 floors | Floor *n* is level *n* (1 to 50) | A post-game replay of the whole level range; no new item levels, so loot stops mattering early |

The rest of this plan assumes **A**: level = 50 + ceil(floor / 5). An item's level is the higher of its drop level and its base's, so the level 60 affixes only roll on drops from level 60, the last five floors. Levels 51 to 60 add some difficulty on their own; the floor itself adds the rest (section 4).

## 4. Floors

**What already exists:** every dungeon is generated by `DungeonDirector` (random seed, rooms, branches, corridors) and painted from a `DungeonTheme` (a mesh library, tile names, an enemy pool, loot rules). A `Waypoint` already carries a level, fixed threats and a count of random ones (`DungeonThreats`), and `WorldState` keeps each instance's seed and roll. A tower floor is one generic dungeon scene that is handed a theme, a level and threats when it loads.

**Each floor is rolled once, when it is revealed** (the floor above the highest one cleared), and saved, so the map can show it before you go in:

| Rolled | How |
|---|---|
| **Look** (`DungeonTheme`) | From the campaign's dungeon themes (`act1_ward`, `act2_stops`, `act2_mire`, `act3_districts`, `act3_heap`, `act3_dangling`, the Act IV Null theme, `page_file`, the Stack Trace's and the Core's), never the same twice in a row. The theme brings its enemy pool, so an Act II look means Act II's enemies, at the floor's level. |
| **Threats** | Floors 1-9: 1 random. 10-24: 2. 25-39: 3. 40-50: 3, plus one Act V threat (FRAGMENTED or PAGED). |
| **Seed** | The layout. Kept until the floor is cleared; a cleared floor rerolls its seed each visit, for farming. |

**Difficulty, per floor *n*:**

| | Floor 1 | Floor 25 | Floor 50 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level | 51 | 55 | 60 |
| Tower pressure (health and damage, added) | +0% | +72% | +147% (3% a floor) |
| Rarity | Act V's weights | more rares | champions common |
| Threats | 1 | 3 | 4 |

There is no per-act bonus on enemies (removed): the tower's difficulty comes from its floor levels (51 to 60), its threats, and tower pressure on top. These numbers are a starting point, to be tuned in play.

**Clearing:** a floor is cleared when its exit opens (all rooms, or the boss on boss floors). Clearing floor *n* reveals *n*+1. Dying ends the attempt; the floor stays uncleared and keeps its roll. No other penalty.

## 6. The Tower Map

A vertical waypoint map: floor 1 at the bottom, 50 at the top, built in code (50 `Waypoint`s in a `WaypointAct` titled THE RECURSION) rather than by hand in a `.tres`.

- Each floor shows its number, level, the look's name, its threat tags (`DungeonThreats` colours) and a boss marker every tenth floor.
- Cleared floors are lit; the revealed floor glows; floors above it are dim, showing only their number.
- Travel works like any waypoint: click a cleared or revealed floor.
- The map opens from the Core's tower entrance and from any floor's exit.

## 6b. Threats Pay (stakes) (built)

Every threat on a floor has a matching reward, shown next to it on the map, so a hard roll is also a rich one. And before entering, anyone can **raise the stakes**: add up to two more threats (from the pool the floor didn't roll) for their rewards. This is open to every ending; it is the tower's core choice, like adding scarabs to a map.

| Threat | Its reward |
|---|---|
| **SWARM** | +40% items dropped (twice the enemies already means more drops; this is on top) |
| **FIREWALLED** | +2 item level on every drop (a level 53 floor drops level 55 items, so the top tiers come sooner) |
| **REFLECTOR** | +30% rarity: drops upgrade a step (normal to magic, magic to rare) more often |
| **FRAGMENTED** (Act V, floor 40+) | Every rare drop has at least one modifier from the **highest tier** its item level allows |
| **PAGED** (Act V, floor 40+) | The floor's last room drops one guaranteed rare at the floor's item level +2 |

**Stacking:** rewards add up, and every threat past the first adds a further +10% to all of them, so four threats pay noticeably more than four times one. Item level is capped at 60, the highest any affix needs. Raised stakes are spent when you enter (the next run of that floor goes back to its own roll).

**How it works:** `Tower.REWARDS` and `Tower.rewards_for` add up a floor's threats; `TowerFloor` hands the result to `DungeonPopulator.rewards`, which scales drop chances (above 100%, a second drop), raises rarity, adds item level (`_drop_level`, capped at 60) and asks `ItemGenerator.generate_item(..., top_tier)` for a top-tier first modifier on rares; PAGED's rare drops on clearing (`drop_bonus_rare`). Stakes ride in the run's seed (five stake bits beside the floor number), so a co-op guest's copy of the floor has them too, and the floor's look and rolled threats are read from the seed without them. The PAGED rare drops where the player stands, not in the last room. From floor 25 a floor already rolls all three ordinary threats, so only the Act V threat it didn't roll is left to stake.

**With Overwrite:** removing a threat also removes its reward, and pinning one carries its reward to the next floor. So patches become a choice between an easier floor and a richer one, not just a way to make the tower easier.

## 7. The Ending Perks

Each ending changes how you play the tower, and each is useful outside it too.

### Merge: the Null's slot, and Null floors (built)

- **Violet rule slot:** every skill gets one more rule (`Endings.bonus_rules`, `SkillProgramLimits.bonus_rules`), shown in the editor's status lines as *"+1 violet rule: merged"*. It's free: it doesn't count against the rank's rule limit. Co-op copies get it through the loadout's limits (`rules`). It's off in Sudo's sandbox, like every other bonus.
- **Null floors:** for a Merge character, 1 floor roll in 4 is a **Null floor** (`Tower.NULL_BIT` in the floor's seed). It gets one more threat it didn't roll (`Tower.null_extra`), +50% items and +20% raised a rarity on top of its threats' rewards, four times the Null stains, and a *NULL FLOOR* notice on arrival. The map shows it in violet. It can't be removed, only climbed or skipped. (Merged Null lines already run in every dungeon after Merge, so they aren't a Null floor's own effect.)

### Overwrite: patch the floor (replaces "weaken the Null") (built)

- **Patches:** you earn one for every 5 floors cleared for the first time, and can hold 3 (`Tower.patches`, saved in `tower`). On the map, before entering an open floor, spend one to:
  - **remove** one of its threats, and its reward (`patch_remove`), or
  - **pin** one of its threats up to the next floor, reward included (`patch_pin`), or
  - **reroll** the floor: a new look and threats (`patch_reroll`).
- Removed and pinned threats live in the floor's seed until it's cleared (then it rolls again), so a co-op guest's copy of the floor has them too.
- kernel.gd stays yours to edit, as today.
- This is the atlas fantasy: Overwrite players shape the climb. It matches the ending: you rewrote AXIOM's rules, now you rewrite each floor's.

### Terminate: REBOOT (New Game+) (built)

- At Character Select, a Terminate character's card has a **REBOOT** button (`SlotButton`, `CharacterSelect`). After a confirmation that lists what's kept and reset, it **reboots the character in its own slot** (`Reboot.make`): the save is replaced, and the one from before is copied to `user://saves/backups/` first.
- **Kept:** name, class, rank and exam grades (the exam quests up to the rank held, completed), the script library, routines, script set names, and kernel.gd's root lines: kernel.gd is open from the start (`KernelScript.unlocked`) and its lines run in every dungeon from Act I (`Endings.runs_everywhere`).
- **Reset:** quests, deeds, waypoints, equipment and backpack (the class's starting kit again), credits, keys, the tower, and the **ending**: a rebooted run can choose a different one (the only way to see another ending on one character).
- **Harder:** enemies have **+100% health and damage per reboot** in every act and the tower (`Reboot.enemy_bonus`, `EnemySpawner`), not in Sudo's sandbox. In co-op the host's reboots decide (sent as `reboot` in the loadout). The Null remembers you: **Null Echoes in Acts II and III** too (`Dungeon._add_reboot_echoes`), announced as *REBOOT: THE NULL REMEMBERS YOU* (no Vesper yet).
- **In the tower:** starts at floor 1, but two floors past the highest cleared are open, so one can be skipped (`Reboot.tower_reveal`).
- A rebooted save carries the deed `reboot:run` (how many reboots), and its card shows *RUN: REBOOT n*.
- **Not checked yet:** every act's quest gates against a save that holds a high rank from the start (exam offers, sealed portals, tutorials). Playtest a reboot through Act I and II first.

## 8. Co-op

- **Whose tower:** the host's. The party climbs the host's floors; guests see the host's map.
- **Clearing:** a floor cleared together counts for everyone in the party, so each player's own tower moves up too (their own save, as quests do today).
- **Perks:** the host's patches and Null floors decide the floor; each player's own violet slot and kernel lines still apply to their own skills, as `party_kernel` already does.
- **Different progress:** a guest who hasn't reached the host's floor can still join it; it doesn't count for their tower until they've revealed it themselves. This stops one player carrying another to floor 50.

## 9. Rewards

- **Drops** at the floor's level (`loot_level_window` 10). The level 55 bases drop from floor 21 (level 55) up; level 45 bases stop past floor 25 (level 55 + the window). Affixes: the item level 55 tier from floor 21, the item level 60 tier only on floors 46-50. That makes the last five floors the farming goal.
- **First clear** of each tenth floor: one script point on every skill (`ScriptBudget`, like clears today), 5 in all.
- **Theorist** (floor 10): +2 script points, as `Ranks` already lists. The trial (`data/exams/theorist.tres`, `ExamSpec.trial`) is sat in Sudo's sandbox with your own gear, skills and scripts: five waves, levels 52 to 56, rarer each wave (normal, magic, magic, rare, a single champion), the last two firewalled (and the champion reflects). No terminal: it starts by itself. Graded on the LOWEST life reached: MERIT 40%, DISTINCTION 70%. Dying fails it (the sandbox pulls you out at 1 health). The gate starts `exam_theorist`; Sudo in the Core hands out the rank.
- **The top:** a title on the character sheet (*RECURSED*), and a line in the epilogue's AFTER page.

## 11. Risks to Check Early

- **Themes:** some looks come with scene-specific extras (Act I's surge decorations, `AreaLighting` light scale, the Page File's PAGED setup). A floor must set lighting and fixed threats from the theme, not the scene. Check every theme loads in the generic scene before anything else (the first test).
- **Mesh libraries:** themes may differ in cell size and tile names; `DungeonTheme` already names its tiles, but each library needs a quick check.
- **Enemy pools at levels 51-60:** early-act enemies were never balanced that high; tower pressure may need a per-theme adjustment.
- **Save size:** 50 floor rolls are small (seed, theme id, threat ids), so this is fine.
